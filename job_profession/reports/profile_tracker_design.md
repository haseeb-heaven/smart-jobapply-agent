# Private profile and manual tracker design

## Scope

The profile vault is local-only under `job_profession/private/` and ignored by
Git. It supports a manual, auditable job-review process, not automatic
submission. The candidate is deliberately positioned as a mid-level,
implementation-focused backend/software developer: maintenance and feature
delivery, APIs, jobs, databases, tests, and integrations.

Personal GitHub AI projects are available only as `personal_open_source`
evidence. They cannot support claims of employer production-AI experience,
AI/ML leadership, seniority, architecture ownership, or management.

## Unresolved fields

The candidate must confirm these fields before an application assistant may
offer a value: current country/city, work authorization and sponsorship for the
specific country, preferred work mode/location limits, canonical professional
years, current/expected compensation by country, telephone number, and current
availability/notice period. The source record preserves supplied values and the
reason each needs reconfirmation. All other sensitive or EEO-type questions are
manual-only unless the candidate explicitly supplies a current approved answer.

## Primary-queue policy

The manual tracker’s **Recommended Jobs** primary queue exposes only jobs with
an evidence-based profile-fit score of **85% or higher** and no hard exclusion.
Lower-fit jobs remain excluded/rejected by default rather than entering the
primary queue. A fit score is an auditable prioritization signal, not a promise
of interview, offer, compensation, eligibility, or job availability.

## Manual application procedure

1. Review the job’s score explanation, evidence labels, gaps, title, company,
   location, and source URL.
2. Reject senior/architect/managerial roles and roles requiring unsupported
   production AI or architecture ownership.
3. Select and truth-check materials using the document manifest; tailor one
   sentence to the actual job description.
4. Manually confirm all pending personal and screening fields. Stop whenever an
   answer would require guessing.
5. Apply through the normal platform flow; the candidate alone performs the
   final submission.
6. Record submitted time, URL, materials, and follow-up date in the tracker;
   preserve history rather than replacing it.
